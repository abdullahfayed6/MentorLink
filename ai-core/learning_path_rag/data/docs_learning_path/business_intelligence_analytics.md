# Business Intelligence and Analytics

## BI Platform Architecture
- **Data Warehousing**: OLAP cubes and dimensional modeling
- **ETL Processes**: Extract, transform, load data pipelines
- **Real-time Analytics**: Streaming data processing and visualization
- **Self-Service BI**: User-friendly data exploration tools
- **Enterprise Reporting**: Automated report generation and distribution

## Analytics and Visualization
- **Descriptive Analytics**: Historical data analysis and trending
- **Predictive Analytics**: Forecasting and machine learning models
- **Prescriptive Analytics**: Optimization and recommendation engines
- **Statistical Analysis**: Hypothesis testing and significance testing
- **Data Storytelling**: Narrative-driven insights and presentations

## Business Metrics and KPIs
- **Financial Metrics**: Revenue, profit, ROI, and cost analysis
- **Operational KPIs**: Efficiency, productivity, and quality measures
- **Customer Analytics**: Lifetime value, acquisition cost, churn analysis
- **Marketing Metrics**: Campaign ROI, conversion rates, attribution
- **Supply Chain Analytics**: Inventory optimization and demand forecasting
