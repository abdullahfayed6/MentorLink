# Financial Technology (FinTech) and Banking Systems

## FinTech Fundamentals

### Digital Banking Evolution
- **Traditional Banking**: Branch-based services and paper processes
- **Online Banking**: Web-based account access and transactions
- **Mobile Banking**: Smartphone apps and mobile-first experiences
- **Digital-Only Banks**: Neobanks and challenger bank models
- **Open Banking**: API-driven third-party financial services

### FinTech Market Segments
- **Digital Payments**: Mobile wallets, peer-to-peer transfers
- **Lending Technology**: Alternative credit scoring and automated underwriting
- **Investment Technology**: Robo-advisors and automated portfolio management
- **Insurance Technology**: Digital insurance and claims processing
- **Regulatory Technology**: Compliance automation and risk management

### Financial Innovation
- **Blockchain in Finance**: Cryptocurrency and distributed ledger technology
- **Artificial Intelligence**: Fraud detection and algorithmic trading
- **Machine Learning**: Credit risk assessment and personalization
- **IoT in Finance**: Connected devices and usage-based insurance
- **Quantum Computing**: Advanced cryptography and risk modeling

## Payment Systems

### Payment Processing
- **Payment Rails**: ACH, wire transfers, and card networks
- **Real-Time Payments**: Instant payment systems and settlement
- **Cross-Border Payments**: International money transfer and currency exchange
- **Merchant Services**: Point-of-sale systems and payment acceptance
- **Payment Orchestration**: Multi-provider payment routing and optimization

### Digital Wallets
- **Mobile Wallets**: Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay integration
- **Cryptocurrency Wallets**: Bitcoin, Ethereum, and altcoin storage
- **Central Bank Digital Currencies**: Government-issued digital money
- **Stablecoins**: Price-stable cryptocurrency for payments
- **Wallet Security**: Private key management and secure storage

### Payment Security
- **PCI Compliance**: Payment Card Industry security standards
- **Tokenization**: Sensitive data protection and secure transmission
- **3D Secure**: Additional authentication for online card payments
- **Fraud Detection**: Machine learning-based transaction monitoring
- **Anti-Money Laundering**: Transaction monitoring and suspicious activity reporting

## Core Banking Systems

### Banking Architecture
- **Core Banking Platform**: Account management and transaction processing
- **Banking APIs**: Open banking and third-party integration
- **Real-Time Processing**: Instant transaction processing and updates
- **Data Architecture**: Customer data management and analytics
- **Legacy Modernization**: Core system transformation and migration

### Risk Management Systems
- **Credit Risk**: Lending decision support and portfolio management
- **Market Risk**: Trading risk assessment and position monitoring
- **Operational Risk**: Process risk identification and mitigation
- **Liquidity Risk**: Cash flow management and funding optimization
- **Compliance Risk**: Regulatory requirement monitoring and reporting

### Customer Relationship Management
- **360-Degree Customer View**: Unified customer data and interaction history
- **Know Your Customer**: Identity verification and due diligence
- **Customer Onboarding**: Account opening and identity verification
- **Customer Analytics**: Behavior analysis and product recommendations
- **Personalization**: Customized products and service offerings

## Regulatory and Compliance

### Financial Regulations
- **Banking Regulations**: Basel III and capital adequacy requirements
- **Consumer Protection**: Fair lending and disclosure requirements
- **Anti-Money Laundering**: KYC and suspicious activity monitoring
- **Data Protection**: GDPR and financial data privacy requirements
- **Cybersecurity**: Financial industry security standards and incident reporting

### RegTech Solutions
- **Automated Reporting**: Regulatory filing automation and validation
- **Transaction Monitoring**: Real-time compliance screening and alerting
- **Risk Assessment**: Automated risk scoring and monitoring
- **Audit Automation**: Compliance audit and documentation systems
- **Regulatory Change Management**: Rule update tracking and implementation

### Emerging Regulations
- **Open Banking**: PSD2 and API-driven financial services
- **Cryptocurrency Regulation**: Digital asset compliance and reporting
- **AI in Finance**: Algorithmic decision-making transparency
- **Data Localization**: Cross-border data transfer restrictions
- **Climate Risk**: Environmental risk assessment and reporting
