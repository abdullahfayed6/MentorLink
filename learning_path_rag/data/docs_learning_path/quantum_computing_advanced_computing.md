# Quantum Computing and Advanced Computing

## Quantum Computing Basics
- **Quantum Bits**: Qubits and superposition principles
- **Quantum Gates**: Logic operations and quantum circuits
- **Quantum Entanglement**: Particle correlation and quantum states
- **Quantum Algorithms**: Shor's algorithm and Grover's search
- **Quantum Supremacy**: Computational advantages over classical computing

## Quantum Programming
- **Qiskit**: IBM's quantum computing development framework
- **Cirq**: Google's quantum circuit library and simulator
- **Q#**: Microsoft's quantum programming language
- **Quantum Simulators**: Classical simulation of quantum systems
- **Quantum Hardware**: Ion traps, superconducting qubits, photonic systems

## Quantum Applications
- **Cryptography**: Quantum key distribution and post-quantum security
- **Drug Discovery**: Molecular simulation and pharmaceutical research
- **Financial Modeling**: Portfolio optimization and risk analysis
- **Machine Learning**: Quantum machine learning algorithms
- **Materials Science**: Chemical compound analysis and material design

## High-Performance Computing
- **Parallel Computing**: Multi-core and distributed processing
- **GPU Computing**: Graphics processing unit acceleration
- **Cluster Computing**: HPC clusters and job scheduling
- **Supercomputing**: Exascale computing and performance benchmarks
- **Scientific Computing**: Numerical simulations and computational science
