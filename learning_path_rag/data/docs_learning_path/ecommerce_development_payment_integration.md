# E-commerce Development and Payment Integration

## E-commerce Platform Development

### E-commerce Architecture
- **Microservices Architecture**: Scalable and modular e-commerce systems
- **API-First Design**: Headless commerce and omnichannel experiences
- **Database Design**: Product catalog, inventory, order management
- **Caching Strategies**: Redis, CDN for performance optimization
- **Search Implementation**: Elasticsearch for product discovery

### Product Management
- **Catalog Management**: Product information and variant handling
- **Inventory Tracking**: Stock levels, reservations, and replenishment
- **Pricing Engine**: Dynamic pricing, discounts, and promotions
- **Category Management**: Product taxonomy and navigation structures
- **Digital Asset Management**: Images, videos, and media optimization

### Shopping Cart and Checkout
- **Session Management**: Cart persistence and user state handling
- **Checkout Flow**: Multi-step checkout optimization
- **Guest Checkout**: Streamlined purchase process for new users
- **Cart Recovery**: Abandoned cart email automation
- **Tax Calculation**: Multi-jurisdiction tax compliance

## Payment Processing

### Payment Gateway Integration
- **Stripe Integration**: Credit card processing and subscription billing
- **PayPal Implementation**: PayPal checkout and payment processing
- **Square Integration**: In-person and online payment processing
- **Payment Method Diversity**: Credit cards, digital wallets, bank transfers
- **Multi-Currency Support**: International payment processing

### Security and Compliance
- **PCI DSS Compliance**: Payment card industry security standards
- **SSL/TLS Encryption**: Secure data transmission protocols
- **Tokenization**: Sensitive payment data protection
- **Fraud Detection**: Machine learning-based fraud prevention
- **3D Secure**: Additional authentication for card payments

### Subscription and Recurring Billing
- **Subscription Models**: Monthly, annual, usage-based billing
- **Dunning Management**: Failed payment retry strategies
- **Proration Handling**: Mid-cycle plan changes and refunds
- **Customer Portal**: Self-service subscription management
- **Revenue Recognition**: Financial reporting and analytics
