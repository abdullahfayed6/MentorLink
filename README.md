### ❔ Project Description:
Students and fresh graduates often struggle to transition from academic knowledge to practical, industry-relevant skills. Many lack access to experienced mentors who can provide guidance, career direction, and networking opportunities. 
Existing learning platforms offer content but fail to provide personalized, structured roadmaps tailored to individual goals. This leads to uncertainty, skill mismatches, and limited job opportunities.
Additionally, students frequently face technical challenges without reliable, accessible support, which slows down their learning journey. Without a solution that combines mentorship, AI-driven learning paths, and instant technical assistance, a significant number of young talents risk being underprepared for the demands of the job market.
